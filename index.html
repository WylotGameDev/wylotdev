The story so far

i make bullshit games, mostly in godot, mostly for jams, because long projects and my attention span are natural enemies. my work leans crunchy and a bit wrong: pixel heavy visuals, ugly in a good way interfaces, sounds like a factory having a bad day. inspired by the work of mike klubnika and wriks, i take tiny weird games very seriously.

the dream: a big project with loads of developers. a real team, real goal, and a shit load of screaming. until then i ship jam games on itch.io steam page someday. mark my words.
